Privacy Policy • Effective September 2026

Welcome to Spot The Chibi: Quick Match Game ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Spot The Chibi does not collect, track, transmit, or store any personal data, personally identifiable information (PII), biometric data, or location data.

  • No account creation or login is required.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or user tracking SDKs are included.
  • No third-party advertisements or ad tracking frameworks are integrated.
  • The App runs completely offline once loaded.

2. Local Device Storage

The App stores non-personal preferences locally on your device using native local storage:

  • High scores / personal best times in Solo Time Attack mode.
  • Sound and Haptic Touch feedback toggles.
  • Selected visual card theme (e.g. Animals, Food, Space, Vehicles, Superheroes).

This data never leaves your device and is erased if you delete the App or clear your browser storage.

3. Children’s Privacy

Spot The Chibi is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, the App fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Family & Kids Category guidelines.
